Which finding is most characteristic of primary adrenal insufficiency?

Reference Strategy

MediSearch is designed to ground answers in legitimate, traceable sources rather than reproduce copyrighted textbooks.

NCBI / PubMed

Biomedical literature and public medical resources.

WHO / CDC

Public-health information and authoritative guidance.

NICE & Guidelines

Clinical guidance and evidence-based recommendations.

Professional Organizations

Medical and nursing organizations where relevant.

Kenyan Authorities

Local health and regulatory sources where applicable.

Textbook Metadata

Legitimate references, editions, chapters and permitted excerpts — not full copyrighted books.

Important safety boundary

MediSearch is an educational and reference platform, not a replacement for a licensed clinician. AI-generated information should not be presented as a definitive diagnosis or individualized treatment plan. Urgent or emergency symptoms require appropriate professional or emergency care.
