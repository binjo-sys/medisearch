## Specialty note structure
Each specialty topic should contain:
1. Learning objectives
2. Specialty-focused definition and scope
3. Etiology / risk factors
4. Pathophysiology where relevant
5. Focused assessment
6. Investigations and interpretation
7. Differential diagnosis
8. Management overview
9. Procedure / safety considerations where applicable
10. Referral and escalation criteria
11. Complications and follow-up
12. Specialty case questions
13. References and source dates
