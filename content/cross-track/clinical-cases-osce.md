# Cross-Track Clinical Cases & OSCE Practice

## Case template
- Patient profile
- Presenting complaint
- History of presenting illness
- Relevant past history
- Medication and allergy history
- Family and social history
- Review of systems
- Focused examination
- Initial observations and vital signs
- Problem list
- Differential diagnosis
- Investigations
- Results
- Interpretation
- Working / final diagnosis
- Management overview
- Nursing responsibilities where applicable
- Clinical Officer responsibilities where applicable
- Medical Officer responsibilities where applicable
- Patient education
- Referral / escalation
- Disposition and follow-up
- References

## OSCE station types
- Focused history
- General examination
- System examination
- Communication and counselling
- Medication counselling
- Clinical procedure preparation
- Emergency response
- Laboratory interpretation
- ECG interpretation
- Imaging discussion
- Discharge counselling

## Track adaptation
The same case should have learner-specific prompts. Diploma Nursing emphasizes assessment, nursing interventions and patient education. University Nursing adds prioritization, evidence appraisal and leadership/research links. Clinical Medicine / CO emphasizes differential diagnosis, investigation and treatment reasoning. Medicine / MO emphasizes diagnostic reasoning, emergency priorities, investigation interpretation and evidence-based management.
